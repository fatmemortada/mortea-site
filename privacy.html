Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Applies to www.mortea.ca

Mortéa is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. We comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws.

1. Who We Are

Mortéa is a beauty and aesthetics discovery platform operated from Montréal, Québec, Canada. For privacy inquiries, contact us at hello@mortea.ca.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Professionals: Business name, owner name, email, phone, address, category, social media handles, profile description, service prices, and photos
  • Clients: Name, email, phone, city, country, booking details, and payment information (processed by Stripe — we do not store card numbers)
  • Contact form: Name, email, and message content
  • Reviews: Reviewer name and review content

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the platform
  • Referring URLs
  • IP address (for fraud prevention and analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account or profile
  • To process bookings and payments
  • To display your profile to clients searching for beauty services
  • To send booking confirmations and platform notifications
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve the platform and user experience
  • To prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

Mortéa does not sell your personal information. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Stripe: Payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Supabase: Database hosting. Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure.
  • Between professionals and clients: When a booking is made, the professional receives the client's name, email, and booking details.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect Mortéa's legal rights.

5. Professional Profile Data

Professional profiles are publicly visible on Mortéa. This includes business name, category, location, description, social links, services, and prices. Profile photos are also public. By submitting a professional profile, you consent to this public display.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained for the duration of account activity
  • Deleted accounts: personal data deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Booking records: retained for 7 years for financial compliance
  • Contact messages: retained for 2 years

7. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA and applicable provincial laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for processing (where applicable)
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise these rights, email hello@mortea.ca.

8. Cookies

Mortéa uses essential cookies for session management and authentication. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure database hosting via Supabase, and payment processing via PCI-compliant Stripe. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Mortéa is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes by email. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Privacy inquiries: hello@mortea.ca
