Toronto's Best Beauty Professionals in 2025

By Mortéa · 7 min read

Toronto's beauty market is one of North America's most dynamic — shaped by the city's extraordinary cultural diversity. Korean, Lebanese, Japanese, Brazilian, and European beauty traditions all coexist and cross-pollinate, creating a scene that's both deeply localised and globally ahead of the curve.

Why Toronto is a beauty capital

The GTA is home to concentrated Korean and East Asian communities that have brought head spa culture, glass skin protocols, and Korean skincare clinics to the mainstream years before they reached other North American cities. At the same time, the Middle Eastern community has made microblading, threading, and lash artistry ubiquitous across Scarborough, North York, and Mississauga. The result: you can find world-class work at every price point.

Most sought-after services in Toronto

  • Japanese head spa — Toronto leads Canada in head spa culture. Specialists in North York, Markham, and downtown Toronto offer full diagnostic sessions with scalp analysis, steam, oil treatment, and massage.
  • Botox and fillers — A dense network of experienced aesthetic doctors and nurse injectors operates across Yorkville, Midtown, and the Annex. Toronto's medical aesthetics scene is well-regulated under Ontario's Regulated Health Professions Act.
  • Laser treatments — Laser hair removal, pigmentation correction, and skin rejuvenation clinics are everywhere. North York and Scarborough have particularly strong clusters of Asian-owned laser clinics.
  • Lash extensions — Classic, hybrid, Russian volume, and Wet Look styles. Independent lash artists with strong Instagram followings operate throughout the city.
  • Microblading and brow artistry — Toronto has some of Canada's most skilled microblading and nano-brow artists, many of whom trained in Korea or the Philippines.
  • Body contouring — Non-surgical fat reduction, RF skin tightening, and EMS muscle sculpting are widely available at premium clinics.

Where to find beauty by neighbourhood

Yorkville: Toronto's luxury beauty corridor. Premium aesthetic clinics, high-end salons, and top-tier injectors. Comparable to Mayfair in London or Marylebone in terms of calibre.

North York & Finch corridor: The heart of Toronto's Korean-influenced beauty scene. Head spas, skincare clinics, lash studios, and nail bars operated by Korean and East Asian professionals.

Markham & Richmond Hill: The GTA's fastest-growing beauty hubs. Large Korean and Chinese communities have created outstanding beauty clusters here — often with more affordable pricing than downtown.

Scarborough & Agincourt: Strong for lash extensions, microblading, and threading. Diverse community of professionals with excellent credentials and competitive pricing.

Mississauga: A mini-city in its own right. Strong across all categories — particularly Botox clinics, laser centres, and full-service salons in the Square One area.

Booking beauty in Toronto safely

For medical aesthetics (Botox, fillers, laser), Ontario law requires that these be performed or supervised by regulated health professionals. Check credentials via the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario or the College of Nurses of Ontario. For beauty treatments, always check portfolios, read Google Reviews, and ask about aftercare and patch testing before any appointment.
