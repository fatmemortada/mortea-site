How it works

📝

Apply for free

Fill in your profile — no credit card, no subscription. Your profile goes live instantly after approval.

💰

Set your own prices

Add your services and set your own prices from your dashboard. You control what you charge.

📍

Get discovered

Clients near you find your profile on the map and discover page. Social portfolio links included.

💳

Clients pay upfront

When a client books through Mortéa, they pay upfront. You receive 90% directly — Mortéa keeps 10%.

🌍

Clients pay in their currency

Stripe automatically converts your prices to the client's local currency — CAD, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, and more.

Fee structure: Mortéa takes 10% of each completed booking. You keep 90%. No monthly fees, no setup costs.

Example: You charge $100 for a facial → client pays $100 → you receive $90 → Mortéa earns $10.

Who can join?
