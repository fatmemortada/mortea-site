Classic vs Hybrid vs Volume Lashes: The Complete Guide

By Mortéa · Expert beauty guide

Lash extensions are one of the most popular beauty treatments worldwide — but with so many styles available, choosing the right set can feel overwhelming. Here's everything you need to know.

Classic lashes

Classic lash extensions are the most natural-looking option. A single extension is applied to each natural lash — giving you a clean, mascara-like effect. If you have a good number of natural lashes, classic extensions will define and lengthen them beautifully.

Best for: Natural lash lovers, first-timers, clients who want a "no makeup" makeup look

Hybrid lashes

Hybrid extensions combine classic single lashes with volume fans — usually a 70/30 or 50/50 mix. The result is a textured, multidimensional look that's fuller than classic but not as dramatic as full volume.

Best for: Clients who want more than classic but aren't ready for full volume. Works well for sparse or inconsistent natural lashes.

Volume lashes

Volume lashes use ultra-fine extensions (0.03–0.07mm) arranged into fans of 2–6 lashes per natural lash. The result is a full, dramatic, fluffy look that photographs beautifully.

Best for: Clients who want a glamorous, full-glam look. Also excellent for sparse natural lashes.

Mega volume

Mega volume takes volume further — fans of 6–16 ultra-fine extensions per natural lash. This is the most dramatic option, delivering an intense, editorial look.

How long do lash extensions last?

Lash extensions shed naturally with your lash cycle — typically every 6–8 weeks. Most clients do infills every 2–3 weeks to maintain fullness. A full set takes 1.5–3 hours depending on the style.

How to choose

The best approach is to book a consultation with your lash technician. They'll assess your natural lashes, discuss your lifestyle (active, swimming, etc.), and recommend the style and curl that suits your eye shape.

Always check your technician's Instagram portfolio before booking — lash work varies enormously by skill level.
