5. Payments and Fees

Mortéa charges a 10% platform fee on all bookings made through the platform. This fee is deducted automatically at checkout. Professionals receive 90% of the booking amount, transferred directly to their connected Stripe account.

Mortéa is free to join for professionals. No subscription fees apply.

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. Mortéa does not store credit card details. By using Mortéa's payment system, you also agree to Stripe's Terms of Service.

Prices displayed are set by individual professionals. Stripe automatically converts prices to the client's local currency at checkout using current exchange rates.
