The Best Beauty Professionals in Montréal Right Now

Montréal has one of the most vibrant and diverse beauty scenes in Canada — a bilingual city where European aesthetics meet North American innovation. From Japanese head spas in Longueuil to laser clinics in Westmount, there's world-class beauty talent across every neighbourhood.

The Montréal beauty scene

Montréal's beauty professionals are known for their craft, creativity, and European sensibility. The city has a strong culture of self-care and personal beauty investment — and a growing demand for medical aesthetics, luxury facials, and specialised wellness treatments.

Services to discover in Montréal

Some of the most sought-after services in Montréal right now:

  • Japanese head spas — A growing specialty in the South Shore and Plateau neighbourhoods
  • Botox and injectables — Luxury clinics in Westmount, NDG, and Laval
  • Lash extensions — Independent technicians across the city with strong Instagram portfolios
  • Laser clinics — Hair removal, skin rejuvenation, and pigmentation treatments
  • Hydrafacials and skin care — Studio-based estheticians across Rosemont and Plateau
  • Permanent makeup — Microblading and powder brows artists based downtown and NDG
