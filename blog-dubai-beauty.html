Dubai's Luxury Beauty Scene: Where to Go and What to Book

By Mortéa · Expert beauty guide

Dubai has established itself as one of the world's premier destinations for luxury beauty and medical aesthetics. With world-class clinics, pioneering treatments, and a clientele that demands the best, the city's beauty scene is unlike anywhere else.

What makes Dubai's beauty scene unique?

Dubai attracts top aesthetic doctors, dermatologists, and beauty professionals from around the world. Regulations around medical aesthetics are strict, which means the standard of care at licensed clinics is high. The city is also a hub for innovation — many treatments reach Dubai before they become mainstream elsewhere.

Most sought-after services in Dubai

  • Botox and dermal fillers — High demand, many world-class injectors based in JBR, Business Bay, and DIFC
  • Laser clinics — Laser hair removal, skin rejuvenation, pigmentation, and fractional treatments
  • Med spas — Full-service wellness and aesthetics combining Eastern and Western treatments
  • Lash and nail studios — Premium studios across Dubai Marina, JBR, and Jumeirah
  • Body contouring — Non-surgical fat reduction, RF, cryo, and sculpting treatments
  • Japanese head spas — Growing rapidly in Dubai, particularly in Downtown and Dubai Hills

Best areas for beauty in Dubai

JBR (Jumeirah Beach Residence): Dense with salons, nail studios, lash bars, and wellness centres. Great for a quick luxury treatment.

Business Bay / Downtown: Home to many premium aesthetic clinics and med spas catering to professionals.

Jumeirah: Quieter, boutique luxury salons and high-end skincare specialists.

Dubai Hills / Meydan: Newer area with modern clinics and luxury wellness destinations.
