Botox 101: What It Is, How It Works, and How to Find a Trusted Injector

Botox is one of the most popular aesthetic treatments in the world — and one of the most misunderstood. Here's what you need to know before your first appointment.

What is Botox?

Botox (botulinum toxin type A) is a purified protein that temporarily relaxes the muscles responsible for facial wrinkles. It's most commonly used to treat frown lines, forehead lines, and crow's feet, as well as for preventative anti-aging, lip flip, brow lift, and jaw slimming treatments.

How does Botox work?

When injected into a specific muscle, Botox blocks the nerve signals that cause the muscle to contract. The result is a temporary relaxation of the muscle — which smooths the overlying skin. Results typically last 3–4 months.

What to expect at your appointment

A Botox appointment typically takes 15–30 minutes. Your injector will assess your facial anatomy, mark the injection points, and administer small injections using a fine needle. Most clients describe the sensation as a mild pinch.

Results take 3–7 days to appear and peak at 2 weeks. There is typically no downtime — most clients return to normal activities immediately.

How to find a trusted Botox injector

Choosing the right injector is the most important decision. Look for:

  • A licensed medical professional (doctor, nurse practitioner, or RN)
  • A strong portfolio of before/after results on Instagram
  • Positive client reviews
  • A consultation before treatment
  • Transparency about product brand and pricing

Avoid anyone offering unusually low prices — underdosing or using counterfeit products is a real risk in the injectable market.
