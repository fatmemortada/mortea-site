Our mission

Beauty discovery done right.

The beauty industry deserves better than generic ads and algorithm-driven feeds. Mortéa is built so clients can find exactly the right professional or clinic near them — with full visibility into their work, location, prices, and real reviews.

For professionals, Mortéa removes the need to fight for attention on platforms not built for beauty. A Mortéa profile means being discoverable by the right clients, at the right time, in the right city.
