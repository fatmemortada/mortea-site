Common questions

Is it really free to join?

Yes — completely free. No credit card, no subscription, no setup fees. You only pay 10% when a client books through Mortéa. If no bookings come in, you pay nothing.

What currencies do clients pay in?

Stripe automatically detects the client's location and shows prices in their local currency — CAD, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, and 135+ other currencies. You always receive payouts in your local currency.

How do I receive my payouts?

Connect your Stripe account from your dashboard. Stripe deposits funds directly to your bank account within 2–7 business days after each booking.

Do I have to use Mortéa's payment system?

No — you can also list an external booking link (Calendly, Jane App, etc.) on your profile. The 10% fee only applies to bookings made through Mortéa's built-in booking system.

Can I set my own prices?

Yes — you set the price for each service from your dashboard. There are no restrictions on what you charge.

What if a client cancels?

Cancellation and refund policies are managed through Stripe. You can set your own policy in your Stripe dashboard settings.
