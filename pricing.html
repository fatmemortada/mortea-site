Common questions

How does the free trial work?

Every new membership starts with a 5-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial ends. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be billed.

When does my profile go live?

After submitting your application, our team reviews it before activating your public profile. This typically takes 24–48 hours. Once approved, you'll appear on Mortéa's map and discovery pages.

Can I upgrade or change my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

What payment methods are accepted?

All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. All payments are secure and PCI-compliant.

What is Mortéa's refund policy?

Memberships can be cancelled at any time. We don't offer prorated refunds for partial months, but you'll retain access until the end of the current billing period.
